How I work

A repeatable path from question to decision.

Every engagement follows the same four moves. The depth changes, not the order.

01 / Discover

Frame the real question

Gather requirements until the ask and the underlying problem line up.

02 / Analyze

Get into the data

Clean, query, and model until the numbers are trustworthy enough to act on.

03 / Recommend

Make the call clear

Turn findings into evidence-based options with their trade-offs spelled out.

04 / Deliver

Hand off something usable

Dashboards, documentation, or a decision memo: whatever gets used.
